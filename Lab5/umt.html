About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia, providing facilities for students to conduct practical activities and research.

In June 1996, the institution became known as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). Later, Terengganu University College (KUT) was established in 1999 and renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM received full university status and was renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu. Today, UMT offers various academic programmes, particularly in marine science, fisheries, technology and environmental studies.
